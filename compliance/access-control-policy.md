# Access control policy

Access is granted by role, least privilege, and business need; protected application actions require server-side verification. Admin wallet access is separate from holder access. GitHub, Cloudflare, provider, and CI access uses MFA where available, named accounts, protected secrets, and prompt removal on role change. Review access at least quarterly.
