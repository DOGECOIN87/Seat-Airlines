# Secure development policy

Security-sensitive changes require review, automated typecheck/tests, dependency and secret scanning, safe headers/CORS checks, and a documented rollback. Tests use synthetic data and no production credentials. Vulnerabilities are triaged by severity with owner and due date. Emergency changes require retrospective review.
