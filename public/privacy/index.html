Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026 · Terms of Service

Seat Airlines collects as little as it can. There are no accounts, no email sign-ups, no advertising trackers and no analytics scripts. This page lists everything the site does store.

What we store on our server

Our server is a Cloudflare Worker with Cloudflare storage.

What is public

Solana balances are public. The site shows each seated holder’s seat, rank, address and balance, and everyone can see published adverts and leaderboard scores. Directory card names and roles are visible to signed-in holders; contact details only to your own section.

What stays in your browser

The site uses your browser’s local storage, not tracking cookies. It keeps your directory session, your best score, sound settings, and any adverts saved only on your device. You can clear these at any time by clearing the site’s data in your browser.

Connecting X

If you connect your X account, X shows you exactly what you are allowing. We use that access only to upload your flight’s picture or video and publish one post, each time you press Share. We do not read your timeline, followers, messages or other X data. Disconnecting deletes the token from our server, and you can also revoke it in X under Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions. X’s handling of your data is covered by X’s Privacy Policy.

Other services the site talks to

  • Cloudflare runs our server, which receives your IP address with each request, as any web server does.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site’s files.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces.
  • Open-Meteo provides the weather outside the window. We send it a rough location worked out from your browser’s time zone, never your real location.
  • Solana RPC and market-data providers supply balances and prices.
  • Your wallet extension or app handles your keys. We never see them.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone except these providers to run the site.

Your choices

You can take down your adverts, edit your card, sign out, disconnect X and clear your browser data at any time. To ask us to delete anything else tied to your wallet, message @SEATAIRLINES on X.

Children

The site is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top.
