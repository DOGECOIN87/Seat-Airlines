How to play

Connect a Solana wallet on the landing page and press Fly. The airliner dives to the deck and the controls are yours: the arrow keys (or WASD) on a computer, a drag anywhere on a phone. The brief is to climb to 10,000 ft — and somewhere on the way, an engine lets go.

  • Boost — the space bar or the round button. The engines still turning light their afterburners for a few seconds: a big shove forward and a hard climb. Three burns in the tank, refilling over time.
  • Updrafts — thermals marked by a cumulus cloud. Fly under one with the wings level and it lifts you.
  • Logos — spinning gold coins ahead. Fly near one and the aeroplane pulls it in: points, and boost back in the tank.
  • Weather — clear air, rain or a thunderstorm, with rough air and lightning.
  • UFOs — now and then one comes for a wing. Climb, dive or bank out of its way.

The multipliers add up: wings level and under 500 ft together is ×2.5, and ×3 in an updraft.
