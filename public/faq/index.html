FAQ

Investing in $SEAT and advertising with Seat Airlines.

Investing

What is the $SEAT token?

$SEAT is the Seat Airlines memecoin on Solana. It powers a 3D browser flight game where market cap is altitude: the bigger the holders, the higher the plane flies. Total supply is 1,000,000,000 SEAT.

Where can I buy $SEAT?

$SEAT launched on pump.fun and can be bought there with SOL. The chart is on DexScreener. As with any memecoin, the price is volatile and can go to zero — do your own research.

What do $SEAT holders get?

Holders sit on the plane's seat map, ranked by bag size — the biggest holders sit up front. Every held seat is a billboard the holder can put a square image advert on. Wallets holding 1,000,000 or more $SEAT unlock the experimental UFO interceptor mode in the game.

Advertising

How can I advertise with Seat Airlines?

There is no ad-sales desk: advertising is holder-native. Hold $SEAT, take a seat on the plane, and put your square (1:1) image on it.

Where do the adverts appear?

On the seat chart and the advert wall directly under the aeroplane on the homepage — no wallet, tab, or click needed to see them. Every visitor and player walks past your billboard.

The game

Is the game free?

Yes. The 3D flight game is free in any modern browser on desktop or mobile. No wallet is needed to fly; connect a Solana wallet to post your scores to the leaderboard.
