The token

What is the $SEAT token?

$SEAT is the Seat Airlines memecoin on Solana. Its public market data flies a 3D browser flight game: market cap is the plane’s altitude. Total supply is 1,000,000,000 SEAT. It is not an investment product, and nothing on the site is financial advice.

Where can I buy $SEAT?

$SEAT launched on pump.fun and trades there for SOL. The chart is on DexScreener. Seat Airlines does not sell the token or handle trades. As with any memecoin, the price is volatile and can go to zero; only buy what you can afford to lose.

Does the Seat Airlines team hold $SEAT?

Yes. The team holds $SEAT from the launch buy, locked on Jupiter Lock, and may hold more in its own wallets, so the team benefits if the price rises. See our interest in the token.

What does holding $SEAT do on the site?

Holders sit on the plane's seat map, ranked by bag size — the biggest holders sit up front. Every held seat is a billboard the holder can put a square image advert on. Wallets holding 500,000 or more $SEAT unlock the F35 fighter in the game, and 1,000,000 or more the experimental UFO interceptor. These are features of the site and can change. Holding $SEAT pays nothing: no share of fees, revenue or profits, and no claim on Seat Airlines.

Advertising

How can I advertise with Seat Airlines?

There is no ad-sales desk: advertising is holder-native. Hold $SEAT, take a seat on the plane, and put your square (1:1) image on it.

Where do the adverts appear?

On the seat chart and the advert wall directly under the aeroplane on the homepage — no wallet, tab, or click needed to see them. Every visitor and player walks past your billboard.

The game

Is the game free?

Yes. The 3D flight game is free in any modern browser on desktop or mobile. Taking the controls needs a connected Solana wallet, but not any $SEAT, and the same wallet signs your scores onto the leaderboard. Scores have no prizes or monetary value.
