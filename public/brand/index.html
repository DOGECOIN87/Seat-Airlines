Brand kit

Brand assets

The logo, badge, mark, colours and type for writing about Seat Airlines. Use them as they are; the rules for doing so are below.

Logo

The primary logo is the horizontal lockup: the badge, and the name in Bruno Ace SC beside it. Use the light lockup on dark grounds and the dark lockup on light ones.

Badge and mark

The badge stands alone wherever there is no room for the name: avatars, app icons, favicons. The flat mark is the badge's seat drawn in one colour, for the aircraft's tail and for anywhere the glossy badge cannot be printed.

Using the logo

Do

  • Leave clear space around the lockup at least half the badge's height on every side.
  • Show the badge at least 24 px tall on screen.
  • Use the files here unchanged, on a ground with enough contrast.
  • Write the name as Seat Airlines in text; the capitals belong to the lockup.

Don't

  • Recolour, redraw, stretch, rotate or crop the badge.
  • Add outlines, glows, shadows or effects to the lockup.
  • Set the name in another typeface, or move the badge and name apart.
  • Use the brand to suggest an endorsement or partnership we have not made.
  • Pair it with price targets, return promises or financial claims.
